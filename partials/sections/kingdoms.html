Six kingdoms

The roaches took five of them

You start in Greenhaven — the only ground still the right colour. Draw a thing, name it, and it comes alive and marches east with you. Every castle you knock down hands the next kingdom back to your pencil.

Greenhaven is the game's. We made the other five names up while drawing the map.

The paper runs east — scroll the map

home

Greenhaven

Your green ground. Draw a thing, give it a name, and it stands up, shakes off the pencil dust and follows you east.

quiet

Dogear Downs

The corner of the page is folded over. The roaches hide in the crease and hope you walk past. Draw an archer and let it do the looking.

ridged

Creasemarch

Ridge after ridge of folded paper. More legs get over them faster than two — so draw a horse, and name it horse.

dark

Smudgewood

Someone dragged a wet thumb through the forest. The roaches like it dark and they brought friends. Bring an army.

sticky

Blotmire

A knocked-over bottle of ink with legs in it. Closed loops come out as armour, so draw your knight in one unbroken line.

last

The Roach Throne

The last castle on the paper. They drew it enormous to frighten you. Bigger is tougher — so draw something bigger, and call it a fire dragon.
