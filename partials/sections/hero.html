The Chronicle of Paper

Being a true account of the war for the six kingdoms, and of Iries, who took back the five that were lost.

Frontispiece: Iries stands alone on hatched ground, the pencil raised like a sword. The dragon he drew stoops overhead and throws fire. Behind him a castle burns.

Copied at Greenhaven from the burnt exemplar. Two leaves are lost before this one.
