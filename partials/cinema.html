How each camera is rigged

This is the creator's half. A camera is an object in the world with a rig — where it sits and what it is told to care about.

Locked
Bolted to a spot. Never moves, whatever happens. Wide, The sky.
Follow
Rides along with one unit, always centred on them. On Iries, Dragon cam.
Tripod
The body stays put; it only turns and punches in to keep its subject framed — like a real camera operator. Low angle, The front.
Crane
Sweeps along a path on a slow loop, high above the line. Crane.
Arrow
Grabs a live arrow out of the volley and falls with it. When it lands, it grabs another. Arrow cam.

Cutting between cameras never touches the simulation — the world has one clock and every camera reads from it. Two people on two cameras are watching the same instant. Recording captures whatever you're cutting to, up to a minute, and saves straight to your machine; nothing is uploaded.
